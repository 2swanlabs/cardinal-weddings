## Brand & Style

The design system channels an understated prairie luxury, evoking the vast horizons, warm open skies, and refined rustic architecture of rural Saskatchewan. The design reflects a modern, whitewashed gambrel barn nestled among open fields—rooted, serene, and intentional. 

The aesthetic is grounded in modern editorial minimalism with subtle warmth:
- Generous breathing room inspired by the wide-open prairie landscape.
- Soft, organic textures translated digitally through ecru canvas surfaces, fine micro-borders, and tactile typography.
- An emotional tone that feels timeless, poetic, celebratory, and intimately hospitable.
- Strict rejection of generic "rustic burlap" or kitsch barn wedding tropes in favor of architectural purity, clean lines, and high-fashion editorial grace.

## Colors

The palette takes direct cues from the physical grounds of the venue: weathered zinc roofing, lush prairie brush, dried fescue grass, and sun-warmed linen.

- **Primary (`#2B322D` - Prairie Slate):** A deep, architectural charcoal green that mirrors dark metal barn trims, iron hardware, and evening shadows across tree lines. Used for primary typography, authoritative accents, and hero actions.
- **Secondary (`#768275` - Sage Spruce):** A muted, dusty prairie foliage tone that delivers soft contrast, iconography accents, subtle highlights, and tag surfaces.
- **Tertiary (`#9E7B66` - Wheat & Ecru Wood):** A warm, sunbaked timber tone reminiscent of warm golden-hour prairie light and oak banquet tables. Used for fine micro-accents and special date badges.
- **Neutral (`#F9F7F2` - Raw Linen):** A delicate, warm cream off-white base that avoids the harsh clinical nature of pure `#FFFFFF`, lending an heirloom stationery quality to every viewport.
- **Surface Accents:** Subdued tint tiers include `#EFECE4` for nested cards and `#DFDDD5` for whisper-light 1px structural framing lines.

## Typography

The typography pairs the high-fashion editorial cadence of **Playfair Display** with the clear, humanist geometry of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Display & Headings:** Rendered in Playfair Display with deliberate optical tracking. Titles must convey the feel of bespoke stationery and luxury lifestyle publications. Italicized styling is reserved for emotional emphasis (e.g., *"unforgettable vows"*, *"the grounds"*).
- **Body & Subtitles:** Handled by Plus Jakarta Sans at lighter weights (300 and 400) to maintain an airy, modern architectural sensibility. Generous line heights ensure relaxed readability.
- **Eyebrows & Labels:** Rendered in uppercase Plus Jakarta Sans with generous tracking (`0.15em`), evoking classic architectural plans and fine-art gallery exhibitions.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts honor the expansive Saskatchewan horizon by prioritizing open breathing space over cramped information density. 

- **Grid Strategy:** A fluid 12-column grid system bounded by a max container width of `1320px` to prevent sprawling lines of text on ultra-wide displays.
- **Canvas Margins:** Wide outer margins (`4.5rem` on desktop, scaling to `1.25rem` on mobile) create a bordered picture-frame feel for photography and event details.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Generous vertical sections separated by deliberate spacing (`space-xl` to double `space-xl`), allowing imagery of the open skies and barn architecture to breathe.
- **Asymmetric Offsets:** Staggered 2-column and 3-column arrangements mimic editorial lookbooks rather than rigid software dashboards.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy avoids heavy drop shadows and artificial skeuomorphism in favor of tactile surfaces and structural linework.

- **Micro-Borders:** Crisp, 1px borders using `#DFDDD5` or low-opacity primary (`rgba(43, 50, 45, 0.08)`) frame images, schedule items, and packages.
- **Tonal Layering:** Depth is conveyed by stepping down from the canvas (`#F9F7F2`) to subtle parchment cards (`#F3EFE8`) and nested details (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Shadow Quality:** When dynamic interaction demands separation (such as booking modal overlays or floating navigation bars), utilize a gossamer, ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 16px 36px -12px rgba(43, 50, 45, 0.06)`.
- **Image Treatments:** Photography sits edge-to-edge within delicately framed containers, with subtle 1px inner borders acting as passe-partout framing.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts crisp architectural lines tempered by very soft corner softening:

- **Level 1 Soft (`0.25rem` / `4px`):** Maintained across buttons, inputs, date pickers, and interactive tiles to mimic precision-milled timber and tailored stationery paper.
- **Deliberate Squares:** Hero imagery, gallery grids, and ambient architectural dividers may intentionally use `0px` border-radius to preserve the sharp, vertical board-and-batten look of the venue's facade.
- **Circular Badges:** Reserved solely for rotating seal stamps (e.g., "Est. 2024", "Saskatchewan Living Skies") and circular photo cameos.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled in `#2B322D` with `#F9F7F2` text, `0.25rem` radius, uppercase tracked label typography (`0.1em`), and generous horizontal padding (`space-lg`). Subtle transition to `#414A43` on hover.
- **Secondary (Linen Outline):** 1px solid `#2B322D`, transparent fill, `#2B322D` text. On hover, fills with delicate slate tint (`rgba(43, 50, 45, 0.04)`).
- **Text Link:** Understated serif or sans with a continuous fine 1px underline positioned with a 4px offset.

### Cards & Event Panels
- Base surface uses `#FFFFFF` or `#F3EFE8` set against the `#F9F7F2` canvas.
- Framed by a subtle 1px `#DFDDD5` border with minimal `0.25rem` rounding.
- Internal padding utilizes `space-lg` to prevent visual clutter around pricing, capacities, and venue features.

### Form Inputs & Date Pickers
- Transparent or `#FFFFFF` fields with bottom-border-only or a fine 1px perimeter in `#D5D1C7`.
- Focus states shift the border to `#2B322D` without garish glow rings.
- Floating labels in `label-uppercase` style that transition smoothly into position.

### Chips & Badges
- Slender pill-like or soft-rectangular tags used for venue traits (e.g., "Air Conditioned Barn", "Bridal Loft", "Up to 250 Guests").
- Background tinted in muted sage (`rgba(118, 130, 117, 0.12)`) with `#2B322D` text.

### Interactive Itinerary & Timeline Lists
- Clean vertical line connectors in `#DFDDD5`.
- Numbered or time-stamped milestone markers displayed in clean, tracked sans typography with Playfair italic subheaders.
