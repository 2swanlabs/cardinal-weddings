Small and intimate is our specialty! We specialize in weddings under 150 people with warm, personalized touches. The barn’s layout naturally creates a romantic, close-knit feel without feeling sparse or overwhelming for smaller guest lists.

The main barn offers 3,200 square feet of thoughtfully crafted space which comfortably accommodates 150 people seated with 5-foot round tables.

For added flexibility, we have an 800 sq ft tent that can accommodate up to 80 guests for overflow, cocktail hour, or an outdoor lounge (available at an extra fee).

For outdoor enjoyment, we offer 5 acres of manicured lawn for formal ceremonies, games, and cocktail receptions, plus 20 acres of natural prairie landscape in the back for serene strolls and breathtaking sunset photography.

Yes! We have 4 dedicated acres of flat parking with additional overflow available. We can comfortably park all vehicles required for your event.

We also strongly recommend arranged shuttle buses for guests. Saskatoon taxi and rideshare services (Uber) easily travel to and from our Vanscoy location (only 7 miles west of Saskatoon).

We hold an exclusive partnership promo code with Best Western Plus Blairmore on the west side of Saskatoon (less than 10 minutes away by car).

Additionally, guests are warmly welcomed to pitch tents or park RVs to camp directly on our designated property area, or choose from several charming local Airbnbs throughout the Vanscoy and Pike Lake area.

Renters are expected to arrange their own volunteers or decorators for initial room configuration, decor setup, and post-event cleanup. However, our on-site venue team is always around during your stay to assist, answer questions, and lend a helping hand.

Prefer to wake up Sunday without lifting a finger? An optional professional clean-up service is available for an additional charge.
