From an intimate tent set up for our own vows to a soaring handcrafted barn resting against the living Saskatchewan skies, everything at Cardinal was born from personal commitment and hands-on dedication.

The Founders

Chris and Michelle

Two small-town Saskatchewan roots, one prairie homestead.

Co-Founder & Host Co-Founder & Craftsman

Michelle grew up in Wilkie and brings a human resources background from hospitality, retail, finance, and healthcare, along with a Bachelor of Commerce and a Master’s degree in Human Resources. Chris, a farm boy at heart from near Leroy, has worked in agriculture, corporate leadership, sales, and insurance, and serves as our resident Jack of all trades around the property. Together they love working in the yard, tending the grounds and the barn, and spending time outside with family, friends, and a whole farmyard of animals, with plenty of guitar, singing, and great music along the way.

Our History

From a personal celebration in a canvas party tent to a permanent architectural haven on the living skies horizon.

The Genesis

Our Wedding & The Party Tent

We originally had our wedding on our property with a party tent and outdoor washrooms that Michelle's dad built for us. Cardinal Weddings & Event Venue grew from there, and we began booking intimate weddings under the canopy.

2023

A Wild Idea & The Barn Build

Early in 2023, we explored the idea of moving a barn from Delisle, but due to extensive costs that idea was put on ice. By spring 2023, we added our first tiny home to the grounds. In the summer of 2023, we got the wild idea to build our very own barn from the ground up—and it quickly took off.

2024 — 2025

Refining the Experience

We are so thankful to all the couples who chose Cardinal for their special day. In the fall of 2024, we installed our comprehensive heating system, commercial water heater, and indoor projector. In spring 2025, we welcomed brand new formal dining chairs for all celebrations.
